## COMPONENT SUBSTITUTION NOTES

### Critical Components (DO NOT SUBSTITUTE):
- **LM3915** - No good alternatives, use exactly this
- **LM7812 / LM7805** - Can use any manufacturer's 7812/7805

### Flexible Components (Can Substitute):
- **LM741** → TL071, TL081 (better performance)
- **LM358** → TL072, LM833 (better for audio)
- **LM339** → 2× LM393 or 4× LM311
- **LM386** → Any small audio amp IC

### Generic Components:
- Resistors: Any brand, carbon film or metal film
- Capacitors: Ceramic/polyester for filters, electrolytic for power
- Diodes: 1N4148 is standard, can use 1N914
- LEDs: Any 5mm LEDs in correct colors
